Troubleshooting and workflow checks

This guide covers GUI 0.2.24. Start with the scan, channel and stage you intend to analyse, then locate the step that produced the unexpected result. Preserve a session checkpoint before replacing preparation or applying a large series fit.

Identify what is being changed¶

If a panel or button seems absent, check View and expandable section headers before changing the model. Compact layouts tab Components/Parameters, may hide the scan browser, and put settings/results under More…. Layout recovery explains reset/show/detach controls and scrolling in preparation/series dialogs.

ActionScope
Choose a row’s DetectorThat scan’s displayed signal and independent channel workspace
Check OverlayA comparison line using that row’s Detector; it does not add a fit target
Preview preparationStaged processing of explicitly checked acquisitions, across mapped detectors
Fit spectrumOne active displayed channel
Stage a series fitExplicit target scans on the source channel/stage; working scans are retained
Apply successful series resultsReviewed successful targets; other scans/channels retain their work
Export fitted CSVSelected rows’ displayed channels and retained successful fit snapshots

The folder-to-results guide connects these steps. Channel and overlay management explains their independent selections.

Import, metadata and channel selection¶

SymptomCheck and next action
Only some folder files loadedRead the failed filenames; folder loading handles native .asc files, not arbitrary CSV files or subdirectories. See import diagnostics.
Review measured-energy order opensInspect the acquisition/sorted previews before accepting a sorted copy. Duplicates and nonfinite values cannot be repaired by sorting.
Incidence is unavailable or unexpectedInspect Source/PV on the Metadata tab. The identified R1 conversion is R1 − 45°; the raw position tuple retains R1 itself.
Set energy differs from the plotted endpointsSet energy reports original mono setpoints. The plot can use measured or calibrated energies, and preparation may restrict PD overlap.
A prepared detector/stage is absentPreparation may not have been applied, or that optional channel failed. Read Channel issues and the row’s preparation status; switching channels does not run processing.
A scan says Previous preparationIt retains older work that could not be replaced by the latest recipe. Review or prepare/refit that channel before using it as current output.

Right-click a scan to record observations in scan notes. Notes are separate from recorded metadata and persist with complete sessions.

Preparation and calibration¶

SymptomCheck and next action
Apply is disabled after moving a windowSettings changed. Run a new successful Preview; old curves are retained for inspection, not as fresh apply-ready output.
The reference panel asks for PreviewPreview performs calibration. Merely opening the panel or changing its inspected scan does not fit a reference.
Reference marker/curve looks wrongCheck the reference channel, feature, search window and target. Parab3 is local three-point interpolation, not a general peak fit. See reference inspection.
No reference fit curve appearsArgextreme and boundary/degenerate parab3 fallbacks can supply a feature energy without a parabola. Read the panel diagnostic.
Separate photodiode processing failsCheck reference selection, mapped channels, I0 values and energy/setpoint overlap. Use the recorded error rather than silently dropping or interpolating rows.
Scale window gives an error or unexpected amplitudeVerify the aligned window and selected signal. Mean and signed trapezoidal area use different denominators; zero/nonfinite denominators are invalid.
Normalisation looks reasonable for one scan but not the seriesEnable comparison overlays and inspect the same prepared stage across representative targets before Apply.

Follow the full preparation procedure for window dragging, all-detector processing and photodiode options. All preparation operates on copies; it does not overwrite acquisition files.

Component controls and fit limits¶

SymptomCheck and next action
Clicking a curve only selects itGrab its orange target, blue μ line or width/tail arrow to drag. See the handle key.
Position, width or tail will not moveCheck Free, links and bounds. A fixed/linked area also prevents height-preserving shape drags. Read Messages for rejected changes.
EMG maximum and μ marker disagreeμ is the underlying Gaussian mean. The maximum generally shifts with tau; see EMG interpretation.
Position-bound lines are absentEnable Show μ bounds, select a visible independent peak, and inspect whether its bounds are infinite/off-screen. Use graphical-bound instructions.
Zoom changes but the fitted interval does notViewing range, fitting domain and peak-position bounds are three separate settings. See fit-domain controls.
An edge position is not editable independentlyIt may follow a peak. Inspect the chosen edge in Link/Unlink edge to μ.
Edge handles are missing or the slope will not dragSelect a visible edge, turn edge subtraction off, and enable Free for post_slope if needed. See edge drag controls.
A dragged peak moves past its former μ limitsPosition drags translate its interval; release and review Min/Max. Fixed states and links remain enforced.
Components are faint, shifted or absentCheck Focus Mode, component visibility and Offset components. Those controls change the view, not fitted contributions.
No Total or residual is visibleTotal appears for peaks/edges; residuals require data plus peaks, edges or linear background. Gain/dE alone do not create those components.

Display settings also explain edge subtraction and sequential comparison colours. A group name or common colour does not create parameter sharing, scale factors or constraints.

Fits, series stages and exports¶

SymptomCheck and next action
Fit reaches its evaluation limitCheck the channel, domain, model, bounds and links before increasing the budget. Use Fit settings; the budget counts residual evaluations.
A fit converges but seems implausibleInspect residual structure, bound hits, compensating parameters and sensitivity to sensible seeds. Convergence alone does not validate a scientific model.
Series trends cannot be enabled for a parameterTrends require an independent per-spectrum parameter and simultaneous fitting. Shared parameters already have one value; linked targets use their controlling source/separation.
Strict trends report infeasibilityCheck order/coordinates, target membership, fixed values and intersected bounds. A named extremum must be among the selected targets.
Pass two is unavailableEvery target needs a successful fitted pass one. Copy-only output, changed first-pass settings or incomplete targets do not supply those seeds.
Pass-two seed or bounds are rejectedReview the averaging pool, seed and scope. Means must be legal for each target; global parameters need a common seed.
Series output is missing from the main plotStaging retains working scans. Review the intended pass and Apply successful results, then return to that target channel.
CSV differs from the edited working curveCSV uses the last successful fitted snapshot, not later trial edits or display transforms. See export scope.
CSV selection omits a scan/channelSelect the row’s intended Detector and ensure it has an applied successful fit with a saved fitted-model snapshot.

Single fits now have a Stop fit control in their progress window. A stopping request waits for the active evaluation to return; trial parameters are discarded. Collection workflows request a safe stop and wait for their worker. Do not close a running worker by force. Series fitting and two-pass review explain staging, invalidation and application.

If a staged scan failed, its spectrum/residual preview is cleared and labelled. Use parameter comparison to inspect gaps and bound hits across the set. After an application interruption, use the recovery choice; an old checkpoint is a partial checkpoint in time, not confirmation that later work was saved.

Keep a useful issue report¶

Record the application version from Help → About…, the operation, scan/channel/ stage, relevant settings and exact message. Messages → History retains recent full workspace feedback; modal import warnings may need recording separately. It is different from the model Undo/Redo history, and neither history persists on restart.

Keep an unedited source acquisition and a complete session if possible. State whether the issue arose before Preview, after Preview, after Apply, during fitting or when exporting. If you supply a screenshot, include the relevant selection and diagnostic. Review files for research information before sharing them.
